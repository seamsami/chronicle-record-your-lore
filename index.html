About

A workspace for imagined worlds.

Chronicle isn't for real-world records — it's a toolkit to capture the shape of your fiction. Whether you build novels, RPG settings, or short stories, Chronicle helps you keep your canon consistent and discoverable.

How to

Record and navigate your lore effectively

  1. Start with an entity: create Characters, Locations, Events, Artifacts, or Concepts.
  2. Give a short summary: a one-paragraph lede helps you (and readers) get oriented fast.
  3. Use structured fields: tags, canonical dates (if any), and relationship links to other entries.
  4. Link heavily: link people to places to events to build a web of references — this is how your world becomes navigable.
  5. Visualize: use timelines for events and maps for locations to see how the world unfolds in time and space.
  6. Version and backup: keep drafts and export snapshots so you can iterate safely.

For templates and deeper tips, read the full guide in this repo (GUIDE.md).

Features

Built for fictional universes

  • Entry templates for Characters, Locations, Events, Artifacts, and Concepts
  • Bidirectional links and tags for fast navigation
  • Timelines and map support to visualize your world
  • Export formats (JSON/Markdown) for backup and portability
